    Volume Oracle v1.0 — Volume Intelligence System¶

    Advanced regime detection and signal generation indicator. Identifies Accumulation/Distribution phases, generates quality-rated BULL/BEAR signals, and provides automated risk management with position sizing, stops, and targets.

    👋

    New to Trading? Start Here

    What Is Volume?

    Volume = How much trading activity is happening at any given time.

    Think of volume like foot traffic in a store:

    • High volume = Lots of people buying/selling (strong interest, significant moves)
    • Low volume = Quiet, not much happening (weak moves, often unreliable)

    In Plain English: Volume Oracle helps you spot when big players (institutions, "smart money") are buying or selling. It's like having X-ray vision for market activity that price alone doesn't show you.

    What You'll See on Your Chart:

    • Volume Bars — Colored by market regime: Green (Accumulation), Red (Distribution), Gray (Neutral), Gold (Weakening)
    • Background Tint — Light green or red showing current market regime
    • Signal Labels — ↑ (BULL) or ↓ (BEAR) with quality stars (⭐⭐⭐)
    • Regime Meter — Compact 6-row table showing state, strength, health, confirmation, flow, and duration
    • HTF Table — Higher timeframe confirmation status (optional)

    How It Works (Simple Version):

    1. Regime Detection analyzes volume to identify Accumulation (bullish) or Distribution (bearish) phases
    2. Signal Generation fires BULL/BEAR signals when conditions align with quality ratings
    3. Risk Management auto-calculates position size, stop loss, and profit targets
    🎓 Why does volume matter?

    Price + Volume = Context

    Price alone doesn't tell the full story. Volume adds context:

    • Price rising + High volume = Strong conviction, sustainable move
    • Price rising + Low volume = Weak move, may reverse soon
    • Price falling + High volume = Strong selling pressure
    • Price falling + Low volume = Weak decline, may reverse soon

    Volume helps you distinguish between strong moves (worth watching) and weak moves (likely to fail).

    Beginner Starting Point:

    • Week 1: Watch the background color and volume bars. Green = Accumulation (bullish), Red = Distribution (bearish).
    • Week 2: Pay attention to BULL (↑) and BEAR (↓) signals when they appear. Note the star ratings.
    • Week 3: Start using the Regime Meter table to understand regime strength and duration.
    • Week 4+: Enable HTF Confirmation to filter signals with higher timeframe alignment.

    Regime Detection¶

    What It Does: Identifies the current market phase using volume analysis, price velocity, trend strength, and divergence detection.

    Regime States:

    RegimeBackground ColorVolume Bar ColorMeaning
    AccumulationLight GreenGreenInstitutional buying phase — bullish bias
    DistributionLight RedRedInstitutional selling phase — bearish bias
    NeutralNone (Gray bars)GrayNo clear regime — selective approach
    WeakeningGold tintGoldCurrent regime losing strength — caution

    Signal Generation¶

    Signal Types:

    • 🟢 BULL Signal (↑) — Bullish conditions confirmed, potential long entry
    • 🔴 BEAR Signal (↓) — Bearish conditions confirmed, potential short entry

    Quality Ratings (⭐): Each signal receives a 0-100% quality score displayed as stars:

    RatingScoreMeaning
    ⭐⭐⭐80-100%Elite quality — highest confidence
    ⭐⭐60-79%Premium quality — good confidence
    ⭐40-59%Standard quality — moderate confidence

    Quality Score Factors: Volume Z-score strength, flow consistency, trend alignment, regime strength

    Risk Management¶

    Auto-Calculated Levels:

    • Position Size — Based on account size, risk %, and stop distance
    • Stop Loss — ATR-based calculation (default: 2.0× ATR)
    • Target 1 (T1) — First profit target (default: 1.5× ATR)
    • Target 2 (T2) — Extended profit target (default: 3.0× ATR)

    Position Sizing Formula: Position Size = Risk Amount ÷ Stop Distance

    Example: $10,000 account × 1% risk = $100 risk. If stop is $50 away, position size = 2 units.

    HTF Confirmation (Optional)¶

    What It Does: Checks higher timeframes for regime alignment before firing signals.

    HTF Table Display: Shows HTF1 and HTF2 regime state, strength %, and alignment status.

    Benefit: Filters out signals that conflict with higher timeframe trend direction.

    Strategy Modes¶

    ModeDescriptionBest For
    Trend FollowingSignals align with regime directionTrending markets, momentum traders
    Mean ReversionSignals at regime extremes (fading)Range-bound markets, contrarian traders
    HybridAuto-adapts based on market conditionsMixed conditions, flexible traders

    Timeframe Compatibility: Works on all timeframes. Signals confirm at bar close (non-repainting).

    Remember: Signals fire only when multiple conditions align: volume spike detected, flow consistency confirmed, regime supports direction. Higher quality ratings = more factors aligned = higher confidence setup.

    📊 System 1: Regime Detection¶

    Visual Display¶

    Chart Background: Colored background overlay on price chart

    Color Coding:
    - Green background: Accumulation regime (bullish)
    - Red background: Distribution regime (bearish)
    - Gray background: Neutral regime (no clear direction)
    - Gold background: Weakening regime (trend losing strength)

    Opacity: Background intensity reflects regime strength (darker = stronger)

    Regime Detection Logic¶

    Accumulation (Green) Detected When:
    - Consistent buying pressure over multiple bars
    - Volume increasing on up moves
    - Price holding support levels

    Distribution (Red) Detected When:
    - Consistent selling pressure over multiple bars
    - Volume increasing on down moves
    - Price failing at resistance levels

    Neutral (Gray) When:
    - No clear directional bias
    - Mixed volume signals
    - Consolidation or low-conviction movement

    Weakening (Gold) When:
    - Current regime losing strength
    - Divergence between price and volume
    - Potential regime change approaching

    Regime Confirmation¶

    Smarter Regime Detection: Regime changes require 2 consecutive bars above threshold AND above-average volume to confirm. This reduces false flips by filtering out noise and low-conviction moves.

    Why It Matters: Multi-bar confirmation + volume validation = fewer false signals.

    Multi-Level Warning System¶

    Four-stage warning progression for regime health:

    StatusMeaningAction
    ✓ STRONGRegime is healthyTrade with confidence
    📉 FADINGEarly warning — regime losing momentumTighten stops
    ⚡ WEAKENINGModerate warning — prepare for potential flipConsider reducing position
    ⚠️ CRITICALUrgent — regime about to flipExit warning triggered

    📊 System 3: Risk Management¶

    Automatic Levels¶

    When a signal fires, Volume Oracle automatically calculates:

    • Entry Price: Optimal entry level
    • Stop Loss: Risk-defined exit level
    • Target 1 (T1): First profit target
    • Target 2 (T2): Extended profit target

    Position Sizing¶

    Based on your configured risk amount:

    Position Size = Risk Amount ÷ Stop Distance

    Example:

    Risk Amount: $150
Entry: $185.42
Stop: $182.15
Stop Distance: $3.27
Position Size: $150 ÷ $3.27 = 46 shares

    📊 System 5: Strategy Modes¶

    Available Modes¶

    Volume Oracle offers three strategy modes to match your trading style:

    Trend Following Mode

    Best for: Trending markets, swing trades
    Signals: Only fires when regime is established (Accumulation or Distribution)
    Logic: Trade with the trend, avoid counter-trend entries

    Mean Reversion Mode

    Best for: Range-bound markets, quick reversals
    Signals: Fires at regime extremes (overbought/oversold)
    Logic: Look for exhaustion and reversal opportunities

    Hybrid Mode (Default)

    Best for: Mixed market conditions
    Signals: Combines trend and reversal signals based on context
    Logic: Adapts to current market structure automatically

    Mode Selection¶

    Market ConditionRecommended Mode
    Strong uptrend/downtrendTrend Following
    Sideways/rangingMean Reversion
    Unsure/mixedHybrid

    🔗 Integration with Other Indicators¶

    💡 Volume Oracle Works Best in Combination

    While Volume Oracle provides powerful regime detection and signal generation on its own, combining it with other Signal Pilot indicators creates high-probability confluence setups. Here are proven integration workflows:

    Volume Oracle + Pentarch (Regime + Timing)¶

    Workflow:

    1. Pentarch signal fires → TD, IGN, CAP, or WRN signal appears on chart
    2. Check Volume Oracle regime → Is the regime aligned with the Pentarch signal direction?
    3. Pentarch + Accumulation regime = validated bullish → Regime confirms institutional support for reversal
    4. Pentarch + conflicting regime = proceed with caution → Signal without regime support may lack follow-through
    5. Expected outcome: Volume Oracle regime validates which Pentarch signals have institutional backing

    Example: Pentarch TD buy signal fires → Volume Oracle shows Accumulation regime (green, 75%) = confirmed bullish reversal with institutional accumulation

    Volume Oracle + Janus Atlas (Regime at Levels)¶

    Workflow:

    1. Price approaches key Janus level → Daily High/Low, Weekly levels, POC, VWAP, or Fibonacci levels
    2. Check Volume Oracle regime at level → What is the regime showing when price tests the level?

    ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid¶

    Learn from Common Pitfalls

    These mistakes are frequently made by new Volume Oracle users. Avoid them to improve your results:

    Mistake #1: Trading Every Signal Without Quality Check¶

    The Problem: Not all signals are equal. A ⭐ (40-59% quality) signal is much weaker than a ⭐⭐⭐ (80-100%) signal. Trading every signal regardless of quality leads to poor results.

    The Fix:

    • ⭐⭐⭐ signals (80-100%) → Full position size, high confidence
    • ⭐⭐ signals (60-79%) → Reduced position size, moderate confidence
    • ⭐ signals (40-59%) → Skip or very small size, low confidence
    • Always check signal quality before entering a trade

    Remember: Quality ratings exist for a reason. Higher quality = more confluence factors aligned = higher probability of success.

    Mistake #2: Ignoring the Regime Background¶

    The Problem: Trading against the regime often leads to losses. A BULL signal during a Distribution (red) regime is fighting the trend.

    The Fix:

    • Green regime (Accumulation) → Favor BULL signals, be cautious with BEAR signals
    • Red regime (Distribution) → Favor BEAR signals, be cautious with BULL signals
    • Gray regime (Neutral) → Wait for regime clarity or use Mean Reversion mode
    • Gold regime (Weakening) → Prepare for regime change, reduce position sizes

    Remember: The regime background tells you the market phase. Trade WITH the regime, not against it.

    Mistake #3: Not Checking HTF Confirmation¶

    The Problem: A signal on your 15m chart might conflict with the 4H regime. Trading against the higher timeframe reduces probability.

    The Fix:

    • Always check if HTF is Aligned before entering
    • HTF Aligned → Signal has higher timeframe support, proceed confidently
    • HTF Not Aligned → Signal is counter-trend on HTF, reduce size or skip
    • The HTF alert will fire when alignment changes

    Remember: Higher timeframe regimes are stronger. A 15m BULL signal is more likely to work if the 4H is also in Accumulation.

    Mistake #4: Holding Through Regime Weakening¶

    The Problem: When the background turns gold (Weakening), the current regime is losing strength. Many traders ignore this warning and hold until the regime fully flips.

    The Fix:

    • Gold background = warning sign → Regime is losing conviction
    • Consider taking partial profits when regime weakens
    • Tighten stops when you see the Regime Weakening alert
    • Don't enter new positions during a weakening regime

    Remember: The gold weakening phase is your warning. Use it to protect profits before the regime flips.

    Mistake #5: Using Wrong Strategy Mode for Market Conditions¶

    The Problem: Using Trend Following mode in a ranging market (or Mean Reversion in a trending market) leads to poor signal quality.

    The Fix:

    • Strong trend visible → Use Trend Following mode
    • Sideways/ranging market → Use Mean Reversion mode
    • Unsure or mixed → Use Hybrid mode (default)
    • Switch modes when market conditions change

    Remember: The Strategy Mode should match current market structure. Hybrid is safe if you're unsure.

    Mistake #6: Not Using the Auto-Calculated Risk Levels¶

    The Problem: Volume Oracle calculates Entry, Stop, T1, T2 for you. Ignoring these levels and placing arbitrary stops/targets reduces the system's effectiveness.

    The Fix:

    • Use the calculated stop loss level (red line)
    • Use T1 for first target (take partial profits)
    • Consider moving stop to breakeven after T1 hit
    • Configure your Risk Amount in settings for proper position sizing

    Remember: The risk levels are calculated based on price structure. They're designed to give you proper risk:reward setups.

    🌫️ When This Doesn't Work Well¶

    Understanding Reduced Reliability Conditions

    Volume Oracle analysis shows reduced reliability in certain market conditions. Recognizing these environments helps set appropriate expectations:

    Extremely Low Volume Sessions¶

    The Condition: During major holidays (Christmas, Thanksgiving), extended weekends, or overnight sessions (especially for stocks/ETFs), volume drops to 10-30% of normal levels.

    What Happens: Volume spikes become meaningless when baseline volume is already low. A 3x spike during overnight trading might represent 1/10th the volume of a normal day session move. Regime detection becomes unreliable when volume data isn't representative of institutional participation. Signals may fire but lack follow-through.

    Context: Volume analysis works because volume represents participation and conviction. When total participation is minimal, volume metrics lose predictive power. It's like analyzing crowd behavior at an empty stadium—the signals exist but aren't statistically significant. Most effective volume analysis occurs during regular market hours when institutional participation is highest.

    High-Frequency Trading Environments (24/7 Crypto)¶

    The Condition: In crypto markets and some forex pairs, algorithmic high-frequency trading (HFT) and market makers create constant volume churn that isn't directional or intentional.

    What Happens: Volume bars show constant high volume but without corresponding meaningful price movement. Volume spikes occur frequently (sometimes every 5-10 bars) due to HFT activity, not institutional positioning. Regime states may flip rapidly between Accumulation and Distribution. Signal-to-noise ratio drops—too many "significant" volume events for any to actually be significant.

    Context: Volume Oracle analyzes volume as a measure of conviction and participation. In HFT-dominated environments, volume reflects algorithmic activity (spread capture, arbitrage, market making) rather than directional conviction. Traditional volume analysis assumptions (high volume = institutional interest) break down. This affects crypto exchanges more than stock markets due to 24/7 trading and lower regulatory oversight of HFT activity.

    Major News & Earnings Events¶

    The Condition: Federal Reserve announcements, earnings reports, FDA approvals, or major geopolitical events cause volume to spike 10x-50x normal levels as news is digested.

    What Happens: Volume Oracle correctly identifies the spike (which is real), but the spike's meaning is different from normal volume spikes. News-driven volume often shows initial panic/excitement that reverses within minutes as the market re-evaluates the news. Regime states shift rapidly during news events as volatility whipsaws price. Signals can fire and immediately move against you as the market reverses multiple times.

    Context: Normal volume spikes indicate smart money positioning or institutional accumulation/distribution over time. News event volume represents reactive trading—everyone processing the same information simultaneously. The first volume spike is often panic, the second is repositioning, the third is rational evaluation. Volume Oracle measures the volume (which is accurate), but interpreting WHICH spike matters requires news context. Best practice: Wait 15-30 minutes after major news for volume patterns to stabilize before trusting Volume Oracle signals.

    Low Float & Illiquid Assets¶

    The Condition: Small-cap stocks, low-volume altcoins, or thinly traded assets where daily volume is minimal and a single large order moves price 5-10%.

    What Happens: Every volume bar looks significant because baseline volume is so low. A single whale order or small fund taking a position appears as a massive volume spike, but it's just one participant, not broad institutional interest. Regime detection identifies apparent accumulation or distribution that represents just one entity's activity. Signals fire but price whipsaws easily because there's no depth of buyers/sellers defending levels.

    Context: Volume Oracle assumes liquid markets where volume represents aggregate participation from hundreds or thousands of participants. In illiquid markets, volume might represent 5-10 participants. The analysis becomes less about "market conviction" and more about "who has a large position." This doesn't invalidate the tool, but changes interpretation: high volume in illiquid assets = specific participants positioning, not broad market agreement. Combine with order book depth analysis if available.

    Opening & Closing Session Volume Spikes¶

    The Condition: First 30 minutes after market open and last 30 minutes before close (especially last 10 minutes) show artificially elevated volume due to market-on-open orders, stop losses triggering, and closing auctions.

    What Happens: Volume Oracle correctly identifies volume spikes, but these spikes are mechanical (index rebalancing, mutual fund closes, retail stop losses) rather than directional conviction. Regime shifts detected during opening/closing often lack significance during mid-session trading. Signals can be misleading—apparent selling pressure in last 5 minutes might be profit-taking, not bearish conviction.

    Context: Not all volume is equal. Opening volume represents overnight news digestion + order backlogs. Closing volume represents fund closes + index rebalancing + retail MOC (market-on-close) orders. Neither necessarily predicts next-session direction. Most reliable volume analysis occurs during mid-session trading (10:30 AM - 3:30 PM ET for US stocks) when volume represents actual directional positioning rather than mechanical flows. Be cautious with signals generated entirely within first/last 30 minutes of session.

    Gap Days & Overnight Gaps¶

    The Condition: Price gaps 3-10% overnight due to news or international market moves, opening at a significantly different price level.

    What Happens: Overnight gaps create discontinuities in volume analysis. Previous regime context may be invalidated when price opens far from the prior close. Signals generated pre-gap may no longer be relevant. The indicator needs time to establish new regime context at post-gap price levels.

    Context: Volume Oracle analyzes continuous price-volume relationships to determine regime. Gaps create breaks in this continuity—price jumps instantly without transitional volume data. After significant gaps (3%+), allow time for new volume patterns to establish. Early post-gap signals may be less reliable until the indicator has processed sufficient data at the new price level. Best practice: Wait for first 15-30 minutes of post-gap trading before trusting Volume Oracle signals.

    Recognizing Reduced Reliability Environments

    Practical Identification:

    • Volume below 40% of 20-day average: Low participation environment—signals less meaningful
    • Volume spikes every 3-5 bars: Likely HFT activity or news event volatility—wait for stabilization
    • Economic calendar shows major events within 30 minutes: Wait for post-news volume patterns to form
    • Asset's daily volume under $10M (stocks) or equivalent: Illiquid—volume analysis less reliable
    • Currently in first/last 30 minutes of session: Mechanical volume flows—use caution
    • Price gapped 5%+ overnight: Prior regime context may be invalidated, wait for new patterns
    • Crypto during low-activity hours (2-6 AM UTC): HFT dominance, low institutional participation

    Remember: Volume Oracle measures what happened—high volume, regime shifts, signal generation. It doesn't tell you WHY volume occurred or WHETHER that volume is meaningful. Context determines meaning: Mid-session institutional accumulation on rising volume = highly significant. Opening gap fill on mechanical volume = less significant. News spike volume that reverses in 10 minutes = noise. The tool provides accurate volume analysis; you provide market context to interpret it correctly.

    ❓ Frequently Asked Questions¶

    Q: What do signal quality stars mean?¶

    A: Stars indicate signal quality: ⭐⭐⭐ = 80-100% (high quality), ⭐⭐ = 60-79% (medium), ⭐ = 40-59% (lower). Higher quality means more confluence factors aligned.

    Q: What do the background colors mean?¶

    A: Green = Accumulation (bullish), Red = Distribution (bearish), Gray = Neutral (no clear direction), Gold = Weakening (regime losing strength).

    Q: How is position size calculated?¶

    A: Position Size = Risk Amount ÷ Stop Distance. Configure your Risk Amount in settings, and Volume Oracle calculates the appropriate position size for each signal.

    Q: Do signals repaint?¶

    A: No. Once a signal fires and conditions confirm at bar close, it remains fixed. Does not change retroactively.

    Q: What is HTF confirmation?¶

    A: HTF (Higher Timeframe) confirmation checks if the larger timeframe regime agrees with your signal direction. For example, a 15m BULL signal is stronger if the 4H is also in Accumulation.

    Q: Which strategy mode should I use?¶

    A: Trend Following for trending markets, Mean Reversion for ranging markets, Hybrid (default) if unsure. Match the mode to current market conditions.

    Q: What does Regime Weakening (gold) mean?¶

    A: Gold background indicates the current regime is losing strength. This is a warning that a regime change may be approaching. Consider tightening stops or taking profits.

    Q: Can I customize the risk levels?¶

    A: Yes. Configure Risk Amount (dollars per trade) and R:R Ratio (for target calculation) in settings. Entry, Stop, T1, T2 are then calculated automatically.

    Q: How do alerts work?¶

    A: 17 alerts across 5 categories: Entry (BULL/BEAR signals), Position (T1 hit, breakeven), Regime (changes, weakening), Health (momentum fading, flow deteriorating, volume drying up, multiple warnings), HTF (alignment changes). Alerts include quality score, regime strength, and trade levels.

    Q: Does Volume Oracle work on all assets?¶

    A: Yes, on assets with real volume data (stocks, futures, crypto). For forex spot (tick volume only), regime detection is less reliable - consider using Harmonic Oscillator instead.
