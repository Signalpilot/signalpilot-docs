    Glossary¶

    Complete reference of Signal Pilot terms, signals, and trading concepts. Updated December 2025 with all current indicator information.

    🎯 Pentarch — Five Cycle Events¶

    Close-confirmed cycle detection across accumulation, markup, distribution, climax, and decline phases.

    🟣 TD — Touchdown (Accumulation Phase)¶

    Color: Purple 🟣
    Phase: Accumulation — Smart money quietly buying after decline
    Meaning: Downward exhaustion detected, early-cycle reversal conditions
    Position: Label appears below candle
    What to do: Prepare for potential reversal — watch for IGN confirmation

    🩵 IGN — Ignition (Markup Phase)¶

    Color: Teal 🩵
    Phase: Markup — Prices climbing as more buyers join
    Meaning: Upward momentum starting, bullish reversal indication
    Position: Label appears below candle
    What to do: Primary long entry signal — stop below IGN candle low

    🟡 WRN — Warning (Distribution Phase)¶

    Color: Yellow 🟡
    Phase: Distribution — Smart money starts selling near top
    Meaning: Price getting stretched, weakening momentum
    Position: Label appears above candle
    What to do: Tighten stops, prepare for potential exit — watch for CAP

    🟠 CAP — Climax (Climax Phase)¶

    Color: Orange 🟠
    Phase: Climax — Peak before reversal
    Meaning: Upward exhaustion detected, late-cycle bearish warning
    Position: Label appears above candle
    What to do: Consider exiting longs, watch for BDN confirmation

    🔴 BDN — Breakdown (Decline Phase)¶

    Color: Red 🔴
    Phase: Decline — Prices fall as sellers dominate
    Meaning: Downward pressure detected, bearish reversal indication
    Position: Label appears above candle
    What to do: Exit longs, potential short entry — stop above BDN candle high

    Pilot Line (PL)¶

    Visual: Thick colored line with semi-transparent ribbon
    Calculation: Double-smoothed EMA (34-period EMA → 3-period EMA)
    Colors:
    • 🟢 Green: Strong uptrend (rising slope, strong momentum)
    • 🔴 Red: Strong downtrend (falling slope, strong momentum)
    • 🟠 Orange: Transitional phase (flat or easing momentum)
    Usage: Primary trend filter — price above green PL = bullish bias, price below red PL = bearish bias

    Regime (Bar Colors)¶

    Visual: Candle body colors — green (bull regime) or red (bear regime)
    Determination: 3-factor voting requiring 2 of 3 votes
    Voting Factors:
    1. Is 34 EMA above 55 EMA? (+1 bull vote)
    2. Is close above Pilot Line? (+1 bull vote)
    3. Is Pilot Line sloping up? (+1 bull vote)
    Usage: Green regime = look for longs, Red regime = look for shorts

    NanoFlow¶

    Visual: Small cross marks (green below lows, red above highs)
    Frequency: 10-30 per day on 1H (vs 3-8 main events)
    Purpose: Micro-momentum shifts between main signals
    Usage: Validate quality of TD/IGN/WRN/CAP/BDN signals — NOT a standalone trade signal

    💧 Plutus Flow — Advanced OBV¶

    Enhanced On-Balance Volume with trend ribbons, divergence detection, and extreme zone signals.

    Flow Ribbon¶

    Visual: Shaded area between OBV flow line and 20-SMA basis
    Colors:
    • 🟢 Green ribbon: Flow above basis = bullish accumulation
    • 🔴 Red ribbon: Flow below basis = bearish distribution
    Intensity: Thicker ribbon = stronger trend

    Cross Signals (Green/Red Dots)¶

    Green dot: Flow crosses ABOVE the moving average = bullish momentum shift
    Red dot: Flow crosses BELOW the moving average = bearish momentum shift
    Usage: Trend change confirmation signals

    Breach Signals (White Dots) ⚪¶

    Color: White dots (3px)
    Meaning: Flow ENTERED extreme zone (±2σ standard deviation)
    • Breach +2σ = Entered overbought zone
    • Breach -2σ = Entered oversold zone
    What to do: WARNING signal — market in extreme state, prepare for potential reversal

    Exit Signals (Yellow Dots) 🟡¶

    Color: Yellow dots (2px)
    Meaning: Flow LEFT extreme zone (exiting ±2σ)
    • Exit +2σ = Left overbought zone → potential reversal DOWN
    • Exit -2σ = Left oversold zone → potential reversal UP
    What to do: ACTIONABLE signal — reversal likely in progress

    Divergence Labels¶

    Bull Div: Price ↓ but OBV ↑ = Reversal UP coming
    Bear Div: Price ↑ but OBV ↓ = Reversal DOWN coming
    Bull Hid: Hidden bullish divergence = Trend continuation UP
    Bear Hid: Hidden bearish divergence = Trend continuation DOWN
    Note: Labels appear on bar close — non-repainting

    📊 Volume Oracle — Volume Intelligence¶

    5-system volume intelligence: Regime Detection, Signal Generation, Risk Management, HTF Confirmation, and Strategy Modes.

    Signal Quality Ratings¶

    ⭐⭐⭐ Elite (80-100%): Highest confidence — all systems aligned
    ⭐⭐ Premium (60-79%): Strong confidence — most systems agree
    ⭐ Standard (40-59%): Moderate confidence — some confluence
    No stars (<40%): Low confidence — conflicting signals

    Volume Regime¶

    Purpose: Identifies current volume environment
    States: Accumulation, Distribution, Trending, Choppy
    Usage: Filter signals based on volume context

    HTF Confirmation¶

    Purpose: Higher timeframe volume trend alignment
    Usage: Trade with HTF volume bias for higher probability

    🎵 Harmonic Oscillator — Composite Momentum¶

    Five-oscillator consensus system combining RSI, Stochastic, CCI, Williams %R, and MFI.

    Voting System¶

    Components: 5 oscillators vote independently
    • RSI (Relative Strength Index)
    • Stochastic
    • CCI (Commodity Channel Index)
    • Williams %R
    • MFI (Money Flow Index)
    Signal: Requires consensus (3+ of 5 oscillators agree)

    Bull/Bear Signals¶

    🟢 Bull Signal: Majority of oscillators show bullish conditions
    🔴 Bear Signal: Majority of oscillators show bearish conditions
    Benefit: Smoother than single oscillator, fewer false signals

    🎮 Omnideck — Everything Indicator¶

    All-in-one indicator combining 10 complete systems.

    10 Systems Included¶

    1. TD Sequential — Exhaustion counts
    2. Squeeze Detection — Volatility compression
    3. SuperTrend — Trend direction
    4. Key Levels — Support/resistance
    5. Divergence — Price/momentum divergence
    6. Momentum — Trend strength
    7. Volume Analysis — Volume patterns
    8. Regime Detection — Market state
    9. Signal Generation — Entry/exit signals
    10. Alert System — Customizable notifications

    📍 Janus Atlas — Levels System¶

    Comprehensive institutional levels system with 60+ automatic levels.

    Level Categories¶

    Timeframe Levels: Daily/Weekly/Monthly highs and lows
    Session Levels: Asia, London, New York session H/L
    Volume Levels: VWAP, POC, VAH, VAL
    Market Structure: FVG, Order Blocks, Breakers
    Opening Range: orH, orL, orMid
    Fibonacci: Auto-drawn retracements (19 anchor options)

    FVG (Fair Value Gap)¶

    Definition: Price gap from buyer/seller imbalance
    Visual: Shaded rectangle on chart
    Types: Bullish FVG (gap up), Bearish FVG (gap down)
    Trading: Entry zones when price returns to fill gap

    OB (Order Block)¶

    Definition: Pre-impulse candle with large order accumulation
    Visual: Highlighted zone on chart
    Trading: High-probability entry zones on revisit
    Validity: Until price closes beyond the block

    Breaker¶

    Definition: Invalidated Order Block
    Behavior: Becomes opposite-direction entry zone
    Example: Bullish OB → broken down → becomes bearish breaker

    Killzones¶

    Visual: Background shading during high-probability windows
    Sessions:
    • Asia: 8PM-12AM ET
    • London: 2AM-5AM ET
    • NY AM: 8:30AM-11AM ET
    • NY Lunch: 12PM-1:30PM ET
    • NY PM: 1:30PM-4PM ET

    📡 Augury Grid — Multi-Symbol Screener¶

    Multi-symbol scanner with MACD confluence scoring.

    Grid Features¶

    Capacity: Monitor up to 8 symbols simultaneously
    Analysis: Trend, momentum, and signal detection per symbol
    Scoring: MACD confluence scoring across symbols
    Usage: Find opportunities across entire watchlist

    📊 Structure & Market Concepts¶

    BOS (Break of Structure)¶

    Bullish BOS: Price breaks above previous swing high → trend continuation UP
    Bearish BOS: Price breaks below previous swing low → trend continuation DOWN
    Trading: Indicates trend strength, anticipate pullback entry

    CHOCH (Change of Character)¶

    Bullish CHOCH: Low holds, breaks previous high → potential reversal UP
    Bearish CHOCH: High holds, breaks previous low → potential reversal DOWN
    Trading: Early reversal warning, await signal confirmation

    HTF / LTF¶

    HTF (Higher Timeframe): Use for overall bias and major levels — HTF trumps LTF
    LTF (Lower Timeframe): Use for precise entry timing within HTF structure

    Confluence¶

    Definition: Multiple signals or levels agreeing
    Example: IGN signal + major support + bullish FVG + Volume Oracle ⭐⭐⭐
    Best Practice: Require 2-3 confluence factors minimum for entry

    🎛️ Filter & Regime Concepts¶

    LOK / SOK¶

    LOK (Long OK): Regime filter = long trades favorable (trend-qualified, not in squeeze)
    SOK (Short OK): Regime filter = short trades favorable (trend-qualified, not in squeeze)
    Usage: Focus on IGN during LOK, BDN during SOK

    Squeeze¶

    Definition: Low volatility period (tight Bollinger Bands)
    Meaning: Consolidation before potential breakout
    Trading: Avoid trading during squeeze, wait for expansion

    🚀 Trading Execution¶

    Bar Close / Close Confirmed¶

    Definition: Signal finalized after candle closes
    Importance: Only closed-bar signals are reliable
    Alerts: Configure "Once Per Bar Close"

    Non-Repainting¶

    Definition: Signals never change after bar close
    Signal Pilot Policy: ALL indicators are non-repainting
    Intrabar changes: Normal (not repainting) — signals update during open bar

    Stop Loss Placement¶

    Long entries: Stop below IGN candle low or TD low
    Short entries: Stop above BDN candle high or CAP high
    Best Practice: Trail stops with trend, never move against position

    Risk/Reward Ratio¶

    Formula: (Target - Entry) ÷ (Entry - Stop)
    Minimum: 2:1 recommended
    Example: $200 risk for $600 profit = 3:1 R/R

    📚 General Trading Terms¶

    Timeframe¶

    Examples: 5m (5 minutes), 1H (1 hour), 1D (1 day), 1W (1 week)
    Strategy: Use multiple timeframes — HTF for bias, LTF for entries

    Long / Short Positions¶

    Long: Expecting price to rise — enter low, exit high (IGN entry, CAP/BDN exit)
    Short: Expecting price to fall — enter high, exit low (BDN entry, IGN exit)

    Pullback / Breakout¶

    Pullback: Temporary retracement within trend — entry opportunity
    Breakout: Price moves beyond support/resistance — trend confirmation

    Trend / Range¶

    Uptrend: Higher highs + higher lows
    Downtrend: Lower highs + lower lows
    Range: Sideways between support/resistance — await breakout

    Backtesting / Paper Trading¶

    Backtesting: Test strategy on historical data
    Paper Trading: Simulated trading with fake money
    Best Practice: Backtest → Paper trade → Live with small size

    Quick Reference Summary
    • Pentarch Colors: 🟣 TD (Purple) → 🩵 IGN (Teal) → 🟡 WRN (Yellow) → 🟠 CAP (Orange) → 🔴 BDN (Red)
    • Plutus Flow Dots: ⚪ White = Breach (warning), 🟡 Yellow = Exit (actionable), 🟢 Green/🔴 Red = Cross signals
    • Volume Oracle: ⭐⭐⭐ Elite (80%+), ⭐⭐ Premium (60-79%), ⭐ Standard (40-59%)
    • Harmonic Oscillator: 5 oscillators vote (RSI, Stochastic, CCI, Williams %R, MFI)
    • Omnideck: 10 systems in one indicator
    • Janus Atlas: 60+ automatic levels
    • Augury Grid: 8 symbols scanned simultaneously
